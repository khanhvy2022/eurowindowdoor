# Page Topology - Eurowindow.biz Clone

1. **Header Component (`src/components/Header.tsx`)**
   - Top bar: Language (VN/ENG), Search bar, Logo, "Nhận tư vấn" button.
   - Navigation menu: GIỚI THIỆU, SẢN PHẨM (sub-menu), SHOWROOM, CÔNG TRÌNH TIÊU BIỂU, TÀI LIỆU, TIN TỨC, CHÍNH SÁCH, LIÊN HỆ.

2. **Hero Section (`src/components/HeroBanner.tsx`)**
   - Main banner carousel with 6 promotional slides.

3. **Introduction Section (`src/components/IntroduceSection.tsx`)**
   - Title: "Giới thiệu"
   - Content: History from 2002 introducing uPVC, aluminum doors, green building materials.
   - Image: Layer 211 image (350x350).

4. **Product Section (`src/components/ProductCategories.tsx`)**
   - Title: "Sản phẩm"
   - Main categories (3 large image cards): CỬA NHÔM, CỬA uPVC, CỬA GỖ.
   - Additional products: SẢN PHẨM KÍNH, CỬA TỰ ĐỘNG, CỬA CUỐN.

5. **Ads Banner Section (`src/components/AdsBanner.tsx`)**
   - Mid-page promotional banner image.

6. **News Section (`src/components/NewsSection.tsx`)**
   - Title: "Tin tức"
   - Left side: 4 recent news cards.
   - Right side: Featured news carousel/highlight box.

7. **Footer Component (`src/components/Footer.tsx`)**
   - Company details: Tòa nhà Eurowindow Office Building, hotline, links, social media.

8. **Floating Contact Widget (`src/components/FloatingContact.tsx`)**
   - Quick phone call button, Zalo button, and advice modal request.
